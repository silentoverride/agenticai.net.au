# Prompt Kit: Agentic Commerce Is Becoming a Protocol War
The old checkout page hid a dozen commercial responsibilities behind one human click. Agentic commerce pulls them apart — and if you're building in this space, you need to know which layers you own, which a partner owns, and which nobody owns yet. This kit gives you two artifacts: a responsibility map that exposes your architectural gaps, and a spending authorization spec that gets your agent past finance, legal, and security review.
## How to use this kit
**Prompt 1 (Responsibility Layer Audit)** is the one to run first. It produces a one-page artifact showing exactly where commercial responsibility lives — and doesn't live — in your product. The "nobody owns this yet" rows are where your roadmap gaps and risk exposure hide. Run this in any capable AI assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini all work well.
**Prompt 2 (Agent Spending Authorization Spec)** is the document every builder will eventually need to produce. Finance, legal, and security will demand it the moment an agent goes near real money. Better to write it before they ask. Run this after Prompt 1, or independently when you're preparing to put an agent into production with real spending authority.
Both prompts gather context conversationally — paste them in, answer the questions, and get a finished artifact you can share with your team, your CFO, or your board.

## Prompt 1: The Responsibility Layer Audit
**Job:** Maps every layer of an agentic purchase to a clear owner — you, a named partner, nobody yet, or not applicable — so you can see exactly where your product's risk and roadmap gaps live.
**When to use:** Before architecting an agentic commerce product. Before a strategy review. Before asking for budget. Before going to production with any agent that touches money.
**What you'll get:** A populated responsibility table covering six layers (discovery, authorization, payment credential, settlement, merchant relationship, governance), each with a clear ownership label. Then three pointed follow-up questions targeting your highest-risk unowned layers.
**What the AI will ask you:** What your product or agent does (one sentence), who the buyer is (consumer, business user, or software system), and who the seller is (merchant, API provider, marketplace, or another agent).

## Prompt 2: The Agent Spending Authorization Spec
**Job:** Produces a draft authorization specification for any agent that will spend real money — the document finance, legal, and security will demand before they approve production deployment.
**When to use:** Before putting an agent into production with real spending authority. Before a budget review where an agent's spending capacity will be questioned. When finance or legal asks "what exactly can this thing buy?"
**What you'll get:** A five-section authorization spec (scope, limits, evidence layer, failure handling, escalation thresholds) plus a "Monday morning audit" — five yes/no diagnostic questions you can ask your team right now to find out whether you're flying blind.
**What the AI will ask you:** What the agent does, whose money it spends, the highest single transaction it could plausibly attempt, and who in the organization approves spending at what threshold.
