# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Australian SMB owners, operations managers, and executives running lean teams of 5–50 people. They are busy, pragmatic, and increasingly skeptical of AI hype. They want a clear, specific answer about where AI can save time or create revenue in their actual business — not a generic strategy document or a software subscription pitch.

Their context: manual reporting, slow enquiry response, repeated customer questions, document-heavy admin, owner bottlenecks. They know their workflows but don't need to know how the technology works.

## Product Purpose

Agentic AI runs a structured AI Business Assessment: a 20–30 minute voice intake with Annie, a 48-hour practical opportunity report, and an optional no-charge follow-up consultation. The output is specific quick wins, ranked recommendations, tool options, effort-versus-impact, and estimated value — with larger AI opportunities clearly marked as separate next steps.

The core loop is Assessment → Strategy → Transformation, not software sales. The product helps SMBs adopt AI responsibly and profitably, with real implementations over experimentation.

## Brand Personality

**Practical · Intelligent · Transformative**

- **Practical**: Business outcomes over hype. ROI, process improvement, operational efficiency. Every recommendation traces back to a specific workflow constraint the business owner already feels.
- **Intelligent**: Deep expertise without arrogance. Strategic thinking, systems-oriented problem solving. AI applied thoughtfully rather than recklessly.
- **Transformative**: Meaningful business change — new operating models, not just automation. Moving businesses beyond simple tool adoption into AI-enabled operations.

Voice: experienced guides helping businesses adopt AI responsibly and profitably — not AI futurists selling the next revolution.

## Anti-references

- **Silicon Valley hype**: No neon gradients, floating AI brains, sci-fi robots, "the future is here" messaging, crypto-startup aesthetics, or excessive buzzwords.
- **Enterprise consultancy bloat**: No corporate jargon, generic stock photography, massive walls of text, or emotionally empty polished design.
- **SaaS product marketing**: No dashboard screenshots everywhere, feature comparison tables, or "sign up now" energy. This is assessment → strategy → transformation, not a software subscription.
- **Generic AI agency template**: No purple gradients, robot imagery, floating network nodes, "AI-powered everything," or GPT-wrapper aesthetics.

The north star: "A trusted business transformation consultancy that happens to specialise in AI, not an AI company trying to convince people AI is important."

## Design Principles

1. **Respect the visitor's time.** Every section must answer "What is it? Why should I care? What happens next?" within 30 seconds of scanning. Short paragraphs, strong hierarchy, generous whitespace.

2. **Show the work, not the technology.** Lead with business outcomes (hours saved, revenue impact, process improvement), not with AI architecture or model names. The technology is the how, never the why.

3. **Specific over generic.** Every claim ties back to a named workflow, a concrete tool recommendation, or a measurable outcome. No "streamline operations" without saying which operation and by how much.

4. **Grounded, not futuristic.** The visual language should feel credible, established, and business-focused — more management consultancy than tech startup. Trust through restraint.

5. **Assessment-first, not product-first.** The site sells the assessment and its outcomes. Any mention of larger AI implementation services is clearly scoped as separate, optional, and only explored after the assessment report is complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum across all surfaces.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must disable parallax, animated backgrounds, and non-essential hover animations. Motion must never be required to understand content.
- Colour is never the sole indicator of meaning — always pair with icons, labels, shapes, or text.
- High readability for non-technical audiences: large body text, strong heading hierarchy, short paragraphs, clear information architecture.
- Cognitive accessibility: no technical jargon without explanation, no dense diagrams without plain-language captions, no overwhelming visual complexity.
