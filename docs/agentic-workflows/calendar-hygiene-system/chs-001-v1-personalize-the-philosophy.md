# Personalize The Philosophy

I want you to help me improve my calendar hygiene.

Before you audit anything, explain the philosophy in a way that fits what you know about me, my work, my constraints, my energy, my communication patterns, and the kinds of commitments I tend to carry. Use memory and context I have already given you, but do not invent facts.

Explain why calendar hygiene is not about having a pretty calendar. It is about making my calendar reflect real commitments, protecting meaningful work, reducing avoidable context switching, aligning email/messages/docs with actual time, and preventing bad scheduling decisions before they harden into the week.

Then tell me what you will inspect, what you will not do without approval, and what output I should expect.

Be candid, specific, and practical. Do not moralize. Do not give generic productivity advice.
