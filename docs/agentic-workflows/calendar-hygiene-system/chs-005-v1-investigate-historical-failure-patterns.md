# Investigate Historical Failure Patterns

Source blog URL: `https://promptkit.natebjones.com/20260428-3x9-guide-main`
Original H2 heading: Prompt 5: Investigate Historical Failure Patterns
Document ID: `calendar-hygiene-system-005-v1`
Version: `v1`

Investigate my last 8-12 weeks of calendar history and the last 30-90 days of email/messages/docs for recurring calendar hygiene failure patterns.

Tell me:

- Where I got myself into trouble
- What kept recurring
- What was probably preventable
- What the calendar failed to represent
- Which patterns are still active right now

Compute or estimate:

- Meeting load by day and week
- Longest uninterrupted focus block by day
- Context switches by cognitive mode
- Back-to-back meeting chains
- Meetings accepted with no agenda or unclear outcome
- Recurring meetings with weak evidence of usefulness
- Deadlines discovered fewer than 48 hours before due date
- Work blocks created too close to deadlines
- Missing prep blocks before external/high-stakes meetings
- Missing follow-up blocks after decision-heavy meetings
- Reschedule churn
- Email/message bursts that interrupt focus blocks
- Commitments from messages that never reached calendar/task systems
- Personal commitments squeezed by work commitments

Classify my recurring failure modes:

- Optimistic estimator
- Meeting accepter
- Fragmenter
- Prep debtor
- Follow-up debtor
- Inbox reactor
- Recovery skipper
- Boundary leaker
- Calendar drifter

Be direct, but keep feedback task-focused. Say "Wednesday had 4.5 hours of meetings and no prep block before the client call," not "you are bad at planning."
