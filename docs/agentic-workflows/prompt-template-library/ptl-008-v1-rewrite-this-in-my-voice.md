# Rewrite This in My Voice

Source document: `/home/loki/Documents/nate/Prompt Template Library.md`
Original heading: 8. Rewrite This in My Voice

**Use Case**: Take AI-generated or dry content and rewrite it to sound more like you.

**Prompt**: "Rewrite the following in my tone of voice: clear, sharp, slightly informal, and focused. No fluff, no overexplaining. Use confident but approachable language. Here is the input: \[paste\]"

**Why It Works**: Style transfer works best when you tell it how you think, not just how you sound.

**Ideal Tools**: Claude, ChatGPT
