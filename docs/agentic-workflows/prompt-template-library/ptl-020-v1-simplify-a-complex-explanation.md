# Simplify a Complex Explanation

Source document: `/home/loki/Documents/nate/Prompt Template Library.md`
Original heading: 20. Simplify a Complex Explanation

**Use Case**: Make a technical or strategic concept easy to understand.

**Prompt**: "Explain \[insert topic\] as if you’re talking to a smart 12-year-old. Keep it accurate, but reduce jargon. Use examples or analogies where helpful."

**Why It Works**: Reveals gaps in your own understanding—and makes your ideas legible to others.
