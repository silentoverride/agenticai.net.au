# Learn My Email Style

Source document: `/home/loki/Documents/nate/Prompt Template Library.md`
Original heading: 1. Learn My Email Style

**Use Case**: Personalize your AI-generated emails to sound like you.

**Prompt**: "Analyze the following sample emails to extract my writing style. Identify patterns in tone, formatting, structure, and phrasing. Summarize these as a writing style guide I can reuse for future messages."

**Why It Works**: Instead of telling the AI how to write, you give it examples—grounding its output in your voice.

**Ideal Tools**: ChatGPT, Claude
