## Executive Summary
Affinity Skin Cancer Clinics has established an 8-year foundation of stability in the Nepean area, supported by a robust 10-person team. However, the business has reached a scaling plateau where the owner’s time is disproportionately consumed by manual administrative tasks—specifically inventory stocktakes, doctor invoicing, and marketing content creation. By implementing automated inventory management through Findle and AI-driven content workflows, the clinic can reclaim approximately 10 hours of leadership capacity per week, representing a net annual value of $75,000 and allowing the Practice Manager to pivot from manual oversight to high-value patient acquisition.

## Pain Points
- **Manual Inventory Management** (high, weekly): The owner physically performs stocktakes and manual reordering, a task that tethers leadership to low-value logistics and creates risks of stockouts or over-ordering.
- **Synchronous Doctor Invoicing** (high, weekly): The current workflow requires the owner to 'sit with the bookkeeper' to generate income invoices for the five doctors, creating a double-handling bottleneck where two high-cost roles are required for one administrative output.
- **Manual Marketing Content Production** (medium, weekly): Creating blog and social media content for Facebook and the website is handled manually by the owner, leading to inconsistent output or 'creative drain' on leadership time.

## Quick Wins
